My take

In five years, nobody will be surprised to see a queer founder.

A lot of LGBTQ people skip business because they think it isn't for them, or that it's too hard to break into. They never see anyone like them doing it, so they assume the door is closed.

That belief is the biggest barrier, and it falls apart once you see someone like you actually doing the work. So I want to be that person, and teach what I learn as I go.

Long term, I want to build my own company instead of spending my career in a 9-5.
